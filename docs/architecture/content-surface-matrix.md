# Historical Content Surface Matrix (Pre-Reset)

> This is a historical reference retained for accepted ADR links. It no longer defines local Access
> routes, hosts, APIs, rendering responsibilities, or database writers.

The 2026-11-10 surface hard reset removed the former local content route families and their
controllers and views. The retained model and database files are reported separately in
[`evidence/2026-11-10-surface-hard-reset-M7Q4.md`](../../evidence/2026-11-10-surface-hard-reset-M7Q4.md);
their presence does not imply a current endpoint or writer.

Related decisions such as content persistence and cross-repository rendering remain historical
records in `adr/`. They do not restore a local content surface in Access.
