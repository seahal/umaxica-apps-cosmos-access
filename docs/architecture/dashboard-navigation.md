# Product Navigation Ownership

Access serves the Auth APP/ORG and Visa APP/ORG interfaces, plus the Mission, Flipper, Blazer,
PgHero, Coverband, and Swagger operator tools. It has no local product dashboard or general account
navigation route.

External relying parties own their signed-in dashboards, product hubs, and navigation. Visa's client
registry does not transfer those responsibilities to Access. Auth may link only to retained
Auth/Visa ceremony destinations.

The former in-repository dashboard navigation contract is retired. Persisted account and preference
records remain subject to the separate model/database usage report; they do not create a local UI
contract.
